AI Proposal Prompts for Freelancers

A useful proposal is not a wall of impressive words. It tells the client what problem you understand, what you will deliver, what is excluded, what it costs, and what happens next.

Prompt: One-page proposal

Act as a clear B2B proposal editor.
Create a one-page proposal from the details below.

Client:
[client type]

Current problem:
[problem]

My offer:
[service]

Deliverables:
[what they get]

Price:
[price]

Timeline:
[timeline]

Rules:
- Do not invent proof
- Do not promise guaranteed outcomes
- Make scope and exclusions clear
- End with one next step

Review checklist

  • Can the client understand the offer in 10 seconds?
  • Are the deliverables concrete?
  • Are exclusions clear enough to prevent scope creep?
  • Is the next step easy to reply to?
