Client Email AI Prompts

Good client email is calm, specific, and easy to answer. AI can help, but only if you give it the decision the client needs to make.

Prompt: Reply to a pre-sale question

Act as a calm, helpful sales assistant.
Reply to this buyer question.

Product or service:
[description]

Buyer question:
[question]

Rules:
- Start with a short acknowledgement
- Give the direct answer early
- Explain who this is and is not for
- End with one low-pressure next step
- Keep it under 160 words

Prompt: Testimonial request

Write a short testimonial request for a buyer.

Product or service:
[name]

Likely result:
[what they completed]

Rules:
- Low pressure
- 3 sentences max
- Include 3 easy questions they can answer
